I spent my first two years as a full-stack developer at product startups like [Innovaccer](https://innovaccer.com) and [Geeks Invention](https://geeksinvention.com), mostly React, Next.js and a lot of test coverage. Then I stepped away from shipping for a while to study full-time, and came back wanting to work on models instead of pixels.

These days I build LLM systems end to end — retrieval, evals, cost tracking, human review. Two are live: [Contract Change-Impact Intelligence](https://change-report.byraj.dev), a multi-tenant contract-diff platform, and [shotgrep](https://shotgrep-demo.vercel.app), video search with published evals.
